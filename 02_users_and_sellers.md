# Users & Seller Ecosystem

## User Statistics

- **Total registered users:** 521
- **Sellers:** 99 accounts (19% of users)
- **Pro sellers:** 1 (the "Silk Road" admin account only)
- **Registration period:** Jan 10 - Mar 21, 2011

## Registration Growth

The site grew slowly at first, then accelerated markedly in early March:

| Period | Registrations | Avg/Day |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| Jan 10-31 | 14 | 0.7 |
| Feb 1-28 | 128 | 4.6 |
| Mar 1-21 | 379 | 18.0 |

The biggest single day was **March 18** with **32 registrations**, followed by March 2 (36) and March 9 (28). Growth in March was likely driven by mentions on Bitcoin forums and word-of-mouth.

## The Admin Account: "Silk Road"

- **UID 1**, registered January 10, 2011
- `is_seller = 1`, `is_pro = 1` (the only pro account)
- Bitcoin address: `1Aqf8rXk6GJ1qHCrFQ6vCQk8Ts1RdhBFRL`
- Account balance at snapshot time: **3,868.56 BTC** (the largest by far)
- The admin was both the site operator AND the first active seller, selling psilocybin mushrooms
- Description: "The Silk Road staff not only builds and maintains this site, we are also sellers on it!"

The admin shipped from the US to worldwide, ran free giveaways ("1 gram shrooms FREE"), and personally handled customer support, bug reports, refunds, and disputes -- all through the same account.

## Notable Early Users

### altoid (UID 2) -- Registered January 15, 2011

The **second user ever registered** on the site. Listed one item: "1/4 oz mid-grade homegrown" marijuana for 50 BTC. Description: "I grow this stuff myself and I love it." Had one transaction (sold to user `superduper`).

This account is historically significant: "altoid" was the username Ross Ulbricht used to promote Silk Road on the Bitcoin Talk forums and the Shroomery.org forums in January 2011. The FBI later traced these promotional posts back to Ulbricht, making this one of the key pieces of evidence in his arrest. The fact that "altoid" is UID 2 -- the very first account after the admin -- strongly suggests this was Ulbricht's own promotional/test seller account.

### 1UP of Canada -- The Thoughtful Vendor

- Registered February 16, 2011
- Prolific Canadian cannabis seller with the most detailed product descriptions on the site
- Offered 12+ marijuana strains (Blueberry, Silver Surfer, Silver Haze, Kush, etc.) at $300-430 BTC/oz
- Championed GPG encryption for address security in forum posts

- Temporarily shut down in mid-March due to "unusual personal circumstances"
- Left a PGP public key in his profile

### maxvendor -- The MDMA/Meth Dealer

- Registered February 5, 2011

- 15 transactions totaling 880 BTC (second highest revenue)

## OPSEC Failures in User Profiles

Several users leaked personal information:

- **NorthernSoul**: Put their email `lufc****@gmail.com` in the Bitcoin address field
- **Phoenix26**: Put their email `itsc********@gmail.com` in the Bitcoin address field  
- **johndeere**: Put what appears to be a bank account number (`91K1311263180512C`) in the Bitcoin address field
- **Goldismoney**: Accidentally revealed their password in plaintext in a PM to the admin when locked out of their account
- Multiple users included PGP public keys in their profiles (good OPSEC), but some included clearnet email addresses in those keys
