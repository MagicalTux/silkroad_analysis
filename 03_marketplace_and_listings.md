## The Admin's Own Listings

The Silk Road admin account was the first and most active seller, with listings focused exclusively on psilocybin mushrooms (Psilocybe Cubensis, "golden teachers"):

- 5g for 29 BTC (~$22)
- 10g for 55 BTC (~$42)
- 1/4 lb for 360 BTC (~$274)
- 1 lb for 999 BTC (~$761)
- 1 oz for 80 BTC (~$61)
- **1g FREE** (promotional, one per customer)

The free gram promotion was a deliberate marketing strategy. The admin posted in the forums: "I also gave away some shrooms last week and am thinking about setting up some kind of contest with shroom samples for prizes."

## Most Notable Listings

### The Altoid Listing (Item 8)
UID 2's (altoid/Ross Ulbricht's) single listing: "1/4 oz mid-grade homegrown, I love this stuff..." for 50 BTC. "I grow this stuff myself and I love it. I have plenty to spare, so I'm listing some on here."

### Stolen Credit Cards (Item 111)
User `spasticplastic` listed "3 US/CAN CC's with full info" for 30 BTC -- the only clearly non-drug contraband in this snapshot. "All numbers have been harvested recently and not sold to more than one buyer."

### Pirated Content (Item 65)
User `anonymous2056` offered to download, burn to DVD, and mail pirated content for 5 BTC per disc.

### Handicap Parking Placard (Item 151)
User `deadfoot` listed a "Handicap Parking Placard, California, Expires 2013" for 30 BTC. "Reclaimed from dumpster."

### Bitcoin Mixing Service (Item 149)
User `muaddib` offered a coin exchange/mixing service for 10.6 BTC per 10 BTC unit, noting "It doesn't take long playing around with block explorer to see that anonymous addresses are traceable."

### Liquor for Minors (Item 55)
User `nvram` offered a "$25 bottle of liquor" for 100 BTC: "Under the age of 21? Trying to throw a party to boost your high school popularity? Starting your alcoholism early?"

### The Farmers Market Referral (Item 144)
User `hookups` sold referral codes for "The Farmers Market" (TFM), another darknet marketplace, for 40 BTC.

### Test Listings
Items 99-100 are test listings by the admin: titles like `"test"jhgkjg` with price 999 BTC, indicating the admin was still actively developing and testing the platform.

## Weapons: The Missing Category

A message from user #17 to the admin (Jan 31, 2011) requested categories for "Firearms, Ammunition, Knives, Other Weapons." The admin confirmed in a forum post that he "had a weapons category up for a while but no one posting." This category was eventually removed due to lack of listings, though the admin expressed willingness to recreate it.
